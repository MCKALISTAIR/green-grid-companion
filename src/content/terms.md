## 2. What GridSense does, and what it doesn't

GridSense shows **forecast and estimated** carbon intensity, generation mix and related information for Great Britain's electricity grid. It also offers suggestions such as "use energy now" or "wait until 2pm".

**Estimates, not advice.** Everything GridSense shows is an estimate:

- Carbon intensity figures are **forecasts and estimates** published by the National Energy System Operator (NESO). They can be revised, delayed or wrong.
- Where NESO hasn't published an "actual" figure, GridSense may **estimate one from the generation mix**, using its own emission factors.
- Your **carbon footprint**, savings, Green Score, "now or wait" answers, appliance and EV-charging comparisons, household-average figures and similar outputs are **GridSense's own calculations**. They use approximations and assumptions, so treat them as a guide only.
- Weather is provided by Open-Meteo and may not match conditions where you are.

GridSense **doesn't give financial, energy-tariff, electrical-safety, environmental-reporting or other professional advice**. It **doesn't control any of your devices**. Don't rely on it:

- for decisions that affect safety (for example, leaving appliances running unattended);
- to choose or switch an energy tariff or supplier;
- for formal carbon or emissions reporting (for example, business or regulatory reporting).

You decide when to use your appliances. GridSense doesn't know your tariff (unless your supplier's data says otherwise), so it can't promise that using energy at a "greener" time will cost you less.
