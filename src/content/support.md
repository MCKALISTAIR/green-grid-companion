# GridSense support

GridSense shows how clean Great Britain's electricity is right now and when it will be cleaner, using data from the NESO Carbon Intensity API.

## Get help

Email [support@getgridsense.com](mailto:support@getgridsense.com) and we'll reply as soon as we can. Please tell us your device and what you were doing when the problem happened.

## Common questions

**Why is my region's number different from the national one?** NESO publishes only forecasts for regions. For Great Britain as a whole it also publishes an after-the-fact figure, estimated from metered power stations. So your region's live number is NESO's forecast, and past regional readings marked 'Est' are our estimate from the fuel mix. Both can differ from the GB figure.

**How do I turn off notifications?** Open Settings in GridSense and switch off the alerts you don't want, or turn notifications off for GridSense in your phone's Settings.

**How do I delete my account and data?** In the app, open Settings and choose Delete account. This deletes your account and the data we hold for it (your saved preferences, notification sign-ups and any Octopus connection) and signs you out on that phone. If you used Sign in with Apple, it also revokes GridSense's access. Settings saved on your phone are cleared when you delete the app.

**Something looks wrong?** If a number looks off, tell us your region and the time you saw it, and we'll check it against NESO's data.
