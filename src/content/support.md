# GridSense support

GridSense shows how clean Great Britain's electricity is right now and when it will be cleaner, using data from the NESO Carbon Intensity API.

## Get help

Email **support@gridsense.app** (placeholder, address to be confirmed) and we'll reply as soon as we can. Please tell us your device and what you were doing when the problem happened.

## Common questions

**Why is my region's number different from the national one?** Regional figures are forecasts from NESO, so they can differ from the national actuals.

**How do I turn off notifications?** Open Settings in the app and switch off the alerts you don't want, or turn them off in your phone's Settings.

**How do I delete my account and data?** In the app, open Settings and choose Delete account. This removes your data and, if you used Sign in with Apple, revokes the app's access.
